How to play
  • Win: grab the enemy flag from their base and bring it to your flag stand. Your own flag must be at home for the capture to count. First to 3 captures wins.
  • If a flag carrier is slain, the flag drops. Touch your own dropped flag to send it home (or it returns by itself after 20s).
  • Right-click to move or attack. Q W E R for abilities (hold to aim, release to cast). R unlocks at level 5.
  • Right-click a ripe crop 🥕🌽🌾🎃 and stand still to harvest it for XP and gold.
  • B recall home · P shop (in base) · Tab scoreboard · Space center camera · Y lock/unlock camera · Enter team chat.
  • Each flag is guarded by one Grain Silo, which rebuilds 2 minutes after it falls. Hide in tall grass to ambush — enemies can't see inside unless they're in it too.
